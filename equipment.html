Equipment

Our standard product is LPHT furnaces for high temperature treatment of diamond materials. These furnaces are commonly used for commercial color treatment of brown and off-color CVD diamonds and in the research of materials exposed to high temperature annealing.

Our production also includes flat furnaces. Flat furnaces are furnaces with two flat horizontal heaters positioned at a distance one over another. Flat furnaces are used for processing of flat disk-like samples.

Cylindrical Furnaces

Furnaces LPHT-20V, LPHT-30V and LPHT-40V are high temperature furnaces with cylindrical heaters and cylindrical sample containers. The cylindrical containers can be made of graphite and/or boron nitride. Cylindrical furnaces are the best choice for the processing of small and medium size samples fitting the container size. Cylindrical containers can accommodate up to 100 carats of individual diamonds.

Flat Furnace

The standard flat furnace LPHT-70F can process disks of diameter up to 70 mm and thickness up to 10 mm. A common application of flat furnaces LPHT-70F is the treatment of as-grown “pancakes” of CVD diamond blocks merged together. LPHT treatment of “pancake” allows to improve color of all blocks in “pancake” and to reduce stress between the blocks simultaneously in one process.
